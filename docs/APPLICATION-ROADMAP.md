## Milestone 7.3 — AI-Assisted Strategic Planning MVP

Status: **AI generation remains future work; provider-independent risk gate implemented at the user's request.** See [planning coverage contract and audit](application/PLANNING-COVERAGE-GATE.md). M7.1 is still awaiting acceptance. Source rules, historical observations, parsed-unqualified saves and manual assumptions remain separate. This gate adds no AI provider or native control.

The MVP should turn an objective such as **monarchist Portugal** into a complete, editable strategy proposal: ordered focuses, relevant decisions, research priorities, economic and military preparation, dependencies, contingencies, risks and source-backed explanations of intended outcomes. “Complete” means every requested planning dimension is addressed, with explicit gaps where evidence is unavailable; it does not mean a guaranteed campaign result or exact optimal schedule.

Reuse and extension seams:

- Inputs: objective, country/start date, selected tree, game/index version, DLC/mod context, known observations and user-confirmed assumptions/preferences. Request missing strategic choices rather than silently invent campaign facts.
- Reuse `POST strategy/paths`, `POST strategy/compare`, `GET strategy/references`, `CampaignPlan`, `PlannerReport`, `PlanningSources`, and the existing reviewed plan preview/apply revision checks. Keep the focus sequence in the current plan model. Add a versioned proposal envelope for decision/research/preparation tasks and cross-task dependencies in M7.3; do not force those tasks into focus IDs or replace current persistence.
- Cite stable definition references plus source/hash/version/index revision for each mechanical claim. Reuse `entryMechanics`' structured explanation tree, guards, target scope, statuses and linked helper evidence; do not parse rendered English back into a planning model. Provider prose is advice, never an authoritative effect evaluator. An unsupported part cannot become supported through AI wording.
- Validate every proposed identifier and dependency against the selected source snapshot. Preserve alternatives, conflicts, unavailable values and conditional outcomes. Distinguish source durations from tentative research/preparation timing; do not sum variable-dependent economic effects into fabricated totals.
- Allow users to reorder, replace, remove and annotate steps, correct assumptions, compare alternatives, save/reopen and preview before applying. Regeneration preserves user edits or presents a reviewable diff. Stale profile/index revisions must trigger revalidation.
- Require `POST strategy/review` for every generated or edited candidate. Carry exact source references, assumptions, a review fingerprint and all unresolved critical/high mechanics. Critical gaps veto confident recommendations and numeric rankings; provisional drafts stay editable. Source-valid focus ordering is not whole-strategy completeness. Event choices, civil-war consequences, decision costs and research/preparation dependencies must clear targeted regressions before their domain can be qualified. No provider may self-certify coverage or optimality.
- Configure provider, credentials, data disclosure and request/cost bounds explicitly in that milestone. Keep secrets outside Git. Offline browsing/manual planning must continue working when AI is unavailable. No gameplay command permission is implied by generating or accepting a plan.

Acceptance: one end-to-end monarchist Portugal proposal covers all requested planning dimensions with resolvable citations; invalid IDs, contradictory branches and unsupported outcomes are rejected or identified; user edits and provenance survive restart; changed-source and missing-campaign-state cases remain honest; a fresh isolated Windows package supports personal review. Test with deterministic provider fixtures before live-provider evaluation. Broader country coverage and automatic gameplay remain later work.
